Our Story

How God wrote our love story

01
The Beginning
When we first met

Every love story starts somewhere. Ours began the way all the best ones do — unexpectedly, quietly, in a moment that felt ordinary until it wasn't. Looking back now, we can see God's hand in every detail.

02
Frienemy to Lovers
Not always easy — but always real

It wasn't all smooth sailing. They were friends, confidants — and yes, sometimes frenemies. But that friction was real, and real is the only kind of love that lasts. Through it all, Raech had a prayer list. Keith was already written on her heart — she just didn't know it yet.

03
The Proposal
She said yes

He knew she was it. She knew too. The proposal was just the moment the world found out what they already knew — that this was exactly where they were supposed to be, right on time.

04
August 14, 2026
Forever begins

And now here we are. About to stand before God, our families, and the people we love most — and say the words we've been building toward all along. Beautiful, in His time.

Questions

Things you might be wondering

What time should I arrive? +
Please arrive before 2:00 PM. Doors open at 2:00 PM and the ceremony begins promptly at 3:00 PM. We would love for you to be settled and present before the processional begins — you won't want to miss Raech's entrance.
Is this an adults-only event? +
Yes. We have lovingly planned this as an adults-only evening so that every guest can be fully present, relaxed, and able to celebrate freely with us. We deeply appreciate your understanding and cooperation.
Can I bring a plus one? +
Invitations are addressed to specific guests. We are unable to accommodate additional attendees, but if you have any questions or concerns, you are always welcome to reach out to Keith or Raech directly.
Can I take photos during the ceremony? +
We kindly request that the ceremony be an unplugged experience. Please keep your phones tucked away so you can be fully present with us in this sacred moment. Our official photographers and videographers will be capturing every detail — you will have beautiful memories to look back on.
Where is the venue? +
We are celebrating at Elora's Guesthouse in Bongabon, Nueva Ecija. The ceremony and reception are held in the same beautiful venue — no transfers needed. Please refer to the Get Directions button in the Details section above for the exact location via Google Maps.
What is the RSVP deadline? +
Please confirm your attendance on or before June 21, 2026. Your prompt response allows us to finalize seating arrangements and catering with care. We are so grateful for your thoughtfulness in responding early.
